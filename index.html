🛒 Step by Step

How to Buy on the Stellar Ecosystem

Four minutes, four steps. You self-custody everything.

Create a Wallet

Download a Stellar wallet such as Freighter, Lobstr, Albedo or StellarX. Save your 24-word recovery phrase offline.

Fund with XLM

Buy XLM on any major exchange and withdraw it to your Stellar address (memo/destination tag if required). Keep ~2 XLM for fees & reserves.

Add a Trustline

On Stellar you must “trust” an asset before it appears. Open the token's card and use Add Trustline, or send a tiny amount from the anchor to auto-create it.

Swap on SDEX

Go to the token's Trade link, connect your wallet, and swap XLM → the token. The order book is on-chain; settlement is instant.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are these the real BTC/ETH/SOL blockchains?

No. They are tokenized representations anchored on Stellar by issuer GD4YAI6OLL6UB66JZ2D7T2CTSEFMR7V44HGZNHZX7Y4NIMRDIAP3SUSA, designed for fast, cheap settlement within the Stellar ecosystem.

What does “fixed 600B supply” mean?

Exactly 600,000,000,000 units of each token exist. The issuer has removed mint authority, so no additional tokens can ever be created.

How do I add a trustline?

In Freighter/Lobstr/StellarX, select “Add Asset”, paste the code and issuer, and confirm. The transaction reserves 0.5 XLM, which stays yours.

Can the project be shut down?

The on-chain token entries and the fixed supply cannot be deleted or altered by the issuer. Trading via SDEX continues as long as the Stellar network and liquidity exist.

Is this a guaranteed investment?

Absolutely not. See the risk disclaimer. Tokens can lose all value. Always DYOR.
