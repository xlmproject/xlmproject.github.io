The Network

Why Stellar?

Stellar is a public, open‑source blockchain purpose‑built for moving value between assets and currencies. Its built‑in decentralized exchange (SDEX) and liquidity pools let any two assets trade directly, with path payments finding the best route automatically [[50]].

Anchored assets — like XLM Project's tokens — are issued by a trusted issuer account and held via a trustline, giving you interoperability with the wider Stellar ecosystem at near‑zero cost.

The Immutable Guarantee

Locked Forever. Built to Outlive Hype.

On Stellar, an issuer can set its master‑key threshold to zero. The moment that happens, the account can never sign a new operation again — no extra supply can ever be minted, and flags can never be changed.

XLM Project applies this to every token: a fixed 600,000,000,000 supply, permanently active, immune to dilution or admin tampering. That is what makes these assets well‑suited to a long‑term, hold‑and‑use strategy rather than a short‑term trade.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are XLM Project tokens?

Anchored representations of ADA, BNB, BTC, DOGE, ETH, PEPE, SHIBA, SOL, TRX and XLM, issued on Stellar by GD4Y…SUSA with a fixed 600,000,000,000 supply each.

Are they the same as the original coins?

No. They are tokens on Stellar, not the native coins on their own chains. They live entirely within the Stellar ecosystem.

What does "immutable" mean here?

The issuer's master‑key threshold is set to zero, so no new supply can ever be minted and settings can't be changed — the supply is locked forever.

How do I buy them?

Create a Stellar wallet, fund it with XLM, add a trustline to the token, then swap on StellarTerm or Lumenswap.

Where do the trade links go?

Each card links to its live pair on the Stellar Decentralized Exchange (StellarTerm), with an explorer link for on‑chain details.
